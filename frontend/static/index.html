RailNexus is a prototype. Telemetry, timetable-derived state, reservation and operational inputs are simulated unless an authorized live source is configured. Historical data is used for model training; it is not runtime per-prediction input. The platform provides operational decision support and does not directly control railway signalling, points, interlocking or physical trains.
